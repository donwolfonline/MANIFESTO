Sending a hushed direct message to one trusted friend protects your ego and leaves four quieter teammates stuck on the exact same mystery.

Ask what that cryptic deployment flag actually does in `#engineering` and write down the answer when you get it. Learning in public shrinks the quiet theater of pretending everyone already knows.
