Clever abbreviations feel breezy at 2pm on a Tuesday and turn hostile during a 2am production alert. Rename `procFlag` to `skipRetryAfterCardDecline` even if it makes the line wrap.

Legible code costs twenty extra keystrokes once and pays back every on-call shift after.

## Why vague names compound

A vague identifier forces every future reader to open two extra files to reconstruct its meaning. Multiply that detour by twenty reviews and four incidents, and a three-letter abbreviation becomes the most expensive shortcut in the module.

## Naming booleans and side effects

Name booleans like a direct yes-or-no question, and let functions that write state say so loudly in their verb.
