Every bloated controller starts with someone whispering, *"one tiny `if` statement won’t hurt."* Before you tuck a ninth special case into a 400-line `syncOrder` function, carve out one honest seam with a clear name and a unit test.

You do not need to rewrite the whole module; leave a doorway the next person can actually walk through.

## Finding the natural seam

Look for a cluster of lines that share the same two inputs and compute one clear value without mutating three outer variables. Pull that piece out first, give it a truthful signature, and stop while the diff stays easy to review.
