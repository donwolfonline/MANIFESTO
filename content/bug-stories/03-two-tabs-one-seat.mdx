Registration for an intimate woodworking workshop opened at noon sharp. The handler queried whether seat B12 was free, paused for twelve milliseconds to format a confirmation token, and inserted the reservation.

## What went wrong

Two eager attendees clicked 'Reserve' inside the same fourteen-millisecond window. Both SELECT statements returned zero rows, both INSERT statements succeeded, and both attendees received proud confirmation receipts for the exact same bench.

## The lasting change

Moving uniqueness to a database constraint with an atomic ON CONFLICT guard turned a race condition into a clean, predictable conflict response.
