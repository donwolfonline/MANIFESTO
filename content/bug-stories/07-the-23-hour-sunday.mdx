An appointment reminder helper scheduled follow-up messages by adding `24 * 60 * 60 * 1000` to the patient's timestamp. It worked faithfully for three hundred and sixty-four days a year.

## What went wrong

On the second Sunday in March, clocks jumped forward an hour at 2:00am. Suddenly twenty-four real hours after Saturday at 9:00am landed on Sunday at 10:00am—or 8:00am when subtracting backward for morning reminders.

## The lasting change

Switching from raw millisecond arithmetic to calendar-day addition kept 9:00am pinned firmly to 9:00am regardless of how many hours lived in the night between.
