Every month-end billing run processed customers in tidy pages of fifty, logging green checkmarks from start to finish. Nobody noticed anything missing until a small architecture studio wrote in asking why they had not been billed for two consecutive months.

## What went wrong

The batch worker advanced its cursor with an inclusive upper bound on one line and an exclusive slice on the next. Every customer sitting cleanly at index 50, 100, or 150 fell straight through the seam between pages.

## The lasting change

Our entire unit test suite ran against twelve seeded fixtures—never once reaching fifty-one. Adding a single boundary test with fifty-one records exposed the bug in four milliseconds.
