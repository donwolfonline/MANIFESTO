Quarterly tax reports took four seconds to aggregate, so we added a tidy sixty-second memo cache in front of the endpoint. Load tests looked marvelous—response times fell from four seconds to nine milliseconds.

## What went wrong

Ten minutes after launch, a designer in Berlin refreshed their draft summary and briefly saw a quarterly total formatted in Japanese yen belonging to a print shop in Kyoto that had clicked refresh three seconds earlier.

## The lasting change

The cache key had been `'tax-summary:2024-Q3'` with no workspace prefix. A one-line key helper requiring tenantId at the type level ensured no future cache entry could ever cross a boundary again.
