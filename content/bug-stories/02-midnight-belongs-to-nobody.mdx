A regional delivery scheduler stored pickup dates as plain ISO calendar strings like '2023-10-29'. On the server in Frankfurt everything looked spotless, and every automated check passed.

## What went wrong

When dispatchers in Oregon opened the Sunday route sheet, JavaScript parsed the date-only string as midnight UTC and rendered it in Pacific time—5:00pm the previous afternoon. Drivers loaded crates a full day early.

## The lasting change

Dates without a clock time do not live on a timeline; they live on a wall calendar. Splitting year, month, and day explicitly stopped the browser from inventing a time zone we never asked for.
