The billing worker flipped `isActive` to true when an annual renewal cleared. Six months later, an onboarding refactor reused the exact same column to mean 'clicked the welcome verification link'.

## What went wrong

When a cleanup migration reset unverified sign-ups, it quietly muted twelve hundred long-standing subscribers who had joined years before verification links existed.

## The lasting change

Both halves of the codebase had been completely logical in isolation—and completely incompatible in conversation. Renaming the column to state the exact business fact made the collision impossible to miss.
