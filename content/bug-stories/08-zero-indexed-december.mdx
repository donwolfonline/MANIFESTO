Monthly archive exports accepted a `?month=12` query parameter from the dashboard dropdown. Everything looked healthy from January through November, silently pulling one month ahead without anyone noticing empty future buckets.

## What went wrong

When December arrived, `new Date(2024, 12, 1)` did not throw a range error—it cheerfully normalized month twelve into January 1, 2025, rendering a completely blank chart right during year-end review.

## The lasting change

Wrapping month conversion in one tested helper with explicit `1..12` bounds validation kept zero-indexed Date math quarantined to a single file.
